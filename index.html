1. Official 1–32 Checkers Board Numbering (PDN Standard)

In official American Checkers / English Draughts (WCDF), pieces move exclusively on the 32 dark squares of the 8×8 board. Squares are numbered sequentially from 1 to 32 starting at Black's top rank (squares 1–4) down to White/Red's bottom king rank (squares 29–32):
